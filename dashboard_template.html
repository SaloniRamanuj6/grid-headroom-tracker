Grid Headroom Tracker

How much room is left for new solar, wind and storage on National Grid's Massachusetts distribution feeders. Independent project using public data from the MA System Data Portal.

Warning: source data may be stale

Feeders by how full they are

% full = (connected + pending MW) ÷ feeder limit, where limit = available + connected MW. Over 100% means more projects are waiting than the feeder has room for.

Substations, least room first

MW left counts pending projects as already taking up room.

All feeders, fullest first
